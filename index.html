About

Behind the cut

I work across static and motion design: branding, print, digital and animation. I want every piece to look good and to do a clear job, whether that is explaining an app in thirty seconds or making a logo feel alive.

Today I'm a motion designer at Redcrabs Creative Works in Hyderabad, where I also lead projects and guide the design team. I trained in animation and graphic design, and I like collaborating closely and learning something new on every project.
